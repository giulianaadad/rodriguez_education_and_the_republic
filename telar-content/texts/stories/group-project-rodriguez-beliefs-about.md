---
title: "Rodriguez beliefs about the poor and their education "
---

Rodríguez believes that poor people should have the same opportunity to receive an education as everyone else rather than being treated as a lasting lower class. On page 87, he argues against creating "Ignorant and Poor Classes" and says that these problems can be addressed through "SOCIAL EDUCATION." For Rodríguez, education is not just about learning how to read or write it is a way to change the conditions that keep people poor and away from society. He believes education should begin in childhood because teaching people early can help them form the knowledge and skills needed to join in their communities. This goes back to his bigger argument that a successful republic cannot depend only on wealthy or educated elites. Rather, the whole population needs to be educated so people can put into society and understand their responsibilities. Rodríguez's concern for poor people also shows why he wanted schools to serve people that had been neglected including Indigenous people and abandoned children. In the end, he sees education as a tool for social equality and world development because a country cannot truly be independent if large parts of its population continue to be excluded and uneducated.
