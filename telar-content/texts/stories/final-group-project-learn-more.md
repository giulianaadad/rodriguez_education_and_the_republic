Guamán Poma was writing ultimately for King Phillip III, as Bauer describes the manuscript as a "Written in the form of a petition to Philip III, his chronicle exposes the abuses of the recent colonial authorities" (Bauer 281). This petition is intended for a devout Catholic king, and as Poma describes in his words: "no written records except for the quips and the memories and accounts of some old Indias" (Bauer 284).  European readers would have a difficult time recognizing it as scholarship.
