title: "Learn more"
---

In this brief reflection, Simon Rodriguez is urging the people who receive knowledge to not accept it passively. Through the use  exclamation marks, he emphasizes the importance of questioning where that knowledge comes from before accepting it. In "Ese candelabro que es la imprenta" by Guadalupe Correa, Rodriguez rejects the longing for perfection which threatens against the publishing of books. He says "Bueno es sin duda las obras sean completas y perfectas; pero, tambien, por queserles darle toda su extencion, o por empenarse en perfeccionarlas al extremo, lla cosiedad se queda sin cosas, y los autores sin el premio que esperar. (pg. 249)" He uses books as an example because the people tend to "believe" everythink that is written in texts, so he cautions the people to not merely settle for what its already "known" but to also explore theit own individual knowledge, perceptions, and place in society.
