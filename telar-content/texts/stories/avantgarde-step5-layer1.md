The picture is a page about Brazilian Concrete Poetry and includes a quote from Haroldo de Campos from 1956. On the left side, the page shows the quote in Portuguese and then gives an English translation underneath it. The quote explains that a concrete poem is made from basic parts of language that are organized in a certain way on the page. On the right side, there is a picture of a concrete poetry design with red, gray, and white shapes that curve around each other. Under the picture, it says "Brazilian Concrete Poetry." The page also includes the words "poesia concreta" and "noigandres 4," which connect the image to the Brazilian concrete poetry movement. The page shows that concrete poetry can use both language and visual design to communicate. Instead of only reading the words, the reader can also look at how the poem is presented on the page.
