In the picture is a poem called "Express" by Vicente Huidobro. The poem is about traveling and mentions many different places, including London, Madrid, Paris, Rome, Naples, Zurich, the Amazon, the Thames, the Seine, and the Rhine. It also talks about mountains, rivers, ships, trains, deserts, and the sea. The words are placed in different parts of the page instead of being written in one normal block. Some lines are centered, while others are moved to the side or placed farther apart. The poem also uses phrases like "ONWARD," "SO LONG," and "THEY'LL BE WAITING FOR ME THERE," which make the journey feel like it is continuing. At the bottom, the image says it is from The Selected Poetry of Vicente Huidobro, translated by David M. Guss. Overall, I tink the image shows a poem that uses words and spacing to describe a long journey.
